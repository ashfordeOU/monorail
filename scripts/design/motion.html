Click the Monorail icon in the menu bar. Inside: ⌘[ back, Esc close, D change the drain.
One panel. No popover opens on top of another; the panel itself becomes Battery, then the app, and back.
Springs, not fades. Pages slide with a slight overshoot and the panel resizes to fit, like Control Centre.
Three rings, one glance. Charge, time left against your usual day, drain against your usual, drawn in on open.
A living glow. Particles stream around the rings, faster and warmer as the drain rises; still with Reduce Motion on.

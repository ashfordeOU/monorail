Finding the newest beta…

Monorail for Windows 11 and 10, beta. Portable: no installer and no administrator rights.

  1. Unzip the whole folder anywhere, such as Downloads or Documents.
  2. Double-click Monorail.exe. It sits in the notification area and starts arranging your windows.
  3. The beta isn’t code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may ask the first time: choose More info, then Run anyway.

Didn’t start? Try again or write to contact@ashforde.org. Found something odd? Tell us.

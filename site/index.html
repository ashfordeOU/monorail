Windows

A strip that runs past your screen.

Grid tilers squeeze every new window into the space that's already there. Monorail does the opposite: a new window slides in beside the one you're using, and nothing you're using ever shrinks.

Docking

Drop a window where you want it.

  • Dock to an edgeDrag a window onto another: a highlight shows the half it will take. Dock onto a docked window and that half splits again, as deep as you like.
  • Tab anythingDrop in the middle for a tab group, even across apps. Drag a tab out to tear it off.
  • Resize two at onceDrag the gap between two windows. Undock with ⌃⌥⇧], float with ⌃⌥T, undo with ⌃⌥Z.

FAQ

Good questions.

What is a scrolling window manager?

A window manager that puts your windows side by side on a strip that runs past the edges of your screen. A new window slides in next to the one you're using, and every other window keeps its size: you move along the strip with the keyboard, the trackpad, the mouse or the slider, instead of squeezing everything into one screen or digging through overlapping windows.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature, including the Dashboard, docking, tab groups, saved views, scripting and every display, with no trial, subscription or window limits. There's no licence key to buy, move between Macs or renew: install it on every Mac you own, and every update is free.

Is it safe to install?

Yes. Every release is signed with Ashforde's Apple Developer ID and notarised by Apple, so macOS opens it without warnings. System Integrity Protection stays on, there's no kernel extension, and nothing is injected into the Dock or other apps.

What does the Dashboard show?

Everything your Mac is doing: CPU, memory, GPU, disk, network, battery, fans and heat, sound, ports and every app, with 30 days of history. From version 1.9.1 it also has AI Usage, Switches, Time, Weather, Meeting Notes and, in Monorail, a Developer page for Homebrew and Docker.

Which AI accounts does AI Usage show?

Claude and Codex plan limits, Gemini CLI, OpenClaw and Hermes from their logs, Ollama and LM Studio models on your Mac, and API keys for OpenRouter, DeepSeek, Moonshot (Kimi), LiteLLM, the Claude and OpenAI APIs and any OpenAI-compatible server. Add as many accounts as you like. Keys stay in your Keychain.

Does Meeting Notes record my calls?

Only when you start it, and a red pill stays on screen while it listens. It turns speech into text on your Mac and keeps no audio; nothing is uploaded. Recording people may need their agreement where you are, so tell everyone first.

Does the clipboard keep my passwords?

No. Copies from password managers are never kept, and anything that looks like an API key, token, private key or card number stays hidden until you click to show it. You can also choose apps whose copies are never kept.

Which Macs does it run on?

Every Mac with macOS 13 Ventura or later, on Apple silicon and on Intel: one universal app. Summaries in Meeting Notes need macOS 26 with Apple Intelligence; Liquid Glass appears on macOS 26.

Can I use Monorail on a work Mac without admin rights?

Yes. It installs into your own Applications folder. Stay Awake, Keep me Available, Evening Mode and the display controls work straight away. Arranging every window needs Accessibility, which IT can approve in one step: the guide for IT.

Will it fight my other window tools?

Quit other window managers first; Settings checks for them. Stage Manager is fine to keep: Monorail pauses while it's on and carries on when you turn it off.

Does it work with several displays, projectors and Sidecar?

Yes: each display gets its own strip, an unplugged display's windows come back when you reconnect, and Sidecar iPads fit every slide.

Can I use a mouse instead of a trackpad?

Yes. Hold ⌥ and turn the wheel, use a thumb wheel such as the one on Logitech's MX Master, or the back and forward buttons. The slider above the Dock can show one app icon per slide; click one to go there.

Which languages?

English, German, French, Spanish, Japanese, Chinese (Simplified), Portuguese, Korean and Italian.

I use AeroSpace, yabai or Rectangle. Can I bring my setup?

Yes. Settings → Basics → Import Settings… brings over your shortcuts, gaps, floating apps and workspace rules.

Can AI agents use it?

Yes. Claude Desktop, Cursor, Hermes Agent, OpenClaw and other MCP clients connect through MCP; agents that load skills get a ready-made SKILL.md; and any agent can run monorail say "Slack and Mail side by side". Monorail asks before an agent closes a window or quits an app.

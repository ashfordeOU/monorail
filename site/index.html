How it works

A strip that runs past your screen

Grid tilers squeeze every new window into the space that's already there. Monorail does the opposite.

01

Open a window

It slides in beside the one you're using. Everything else keeps its size.

02

Move along

⌃⌥← →, the mouse wheel, a swipe or the slider: the strip glides just far enough.

03

See it all

⌃⌥O zooms out to every workspace and slide. Drag windows anywhere.

Docking

Drop a window where you want it

Drag a window onto another one. Near an edge it docks beside it; in the middle the two become a tab group. Any apps, as deep as you like. Try it here.

Floating
1

Drag onto an edge of any window. A highlight shows the half it will take; let go and it docks there.

2

Keep going. Dock onto a docked window and that half splits again, as deep as you like.

3

Drop in the middle for a tab group, even across apps. Drag a tab out to tear it off.

4

Drag the gap between two windows to resize both at once.

5

Undock with ⌃⌥⇧] for a slide of its own, ⌃⌥T to float (here, drag to Floating), or ⌃⌥Z to undo.

Windows of one app share a slide. Pair any two, in grids, main + side, tabs or tall slides.

Overview ⌃⌥O

Every workspace on one colourful canvas. Drag to pair, reorder, move or close.

☕

Stay Awake

For an hour, until a time, while something downloads or while an app is open. The download version also keeps a MacBook running with the lid closed: only while plugged in unless you choose otherwise, and it stops if the Mac gets hot.

🌙

Evening Mode

Less blue light from every display, as warm as you like. Turn it on by hand or on a schedule; it fades in over 20 minutes.

🤖

AI agent alerts

A chime when Claude Code or Codex waits for approval. Assistants can drive Monorail over MCP.

🛡️

Safe screen sharing

Private windows step aside the moment you share in Zoom, Teams or Meet.

🎯

Focus and deep work ⌃⌥B

Dim everything but your slide, hide distracting apps, and work in pomodoro blocks.

🖥️

Displays that remember

Unplug and replug: every window goes back where it was. A layout for each desk.

⌨️

No chords needed

Tap right ⌥ for slide mode, or use Vim keys, a MIDI pad, a game controller or your phone.

🪟

Dock anything, nest anywhere

Split a slide by dragging, group windows from different apps into tabs, and resize two windows at once from the gap between them.

📍

Saved views ⌃⌥⌘1

Nine places per display to jump back to, plus back and forward through where you've been, like a browser.

📐

Draw your own layouts

The zone editor splits a slide any way you like. Use it on any slide from its menu.

📦

Switch in a minute

Imports your shortcuts, gaps and app rules from AeroSpace, yabai and skhd, or Rectangle.

🏷️

Tags

Keep a window on the workspaces you choose, or show another workspace alongside this one.

🔌

Built to be scripted

monorail:// links, a CLI with a live event stream, a local API, MCP for AI assistants, and shareable layout links.

Privacy

Your windows stay yours

No account. Nothing personal.

Window titles, contents and files never leave your Mac. Besides update checks and a list of app quirks, Monorail sends only anonymous daily counts (versions, Mac model, features on), and you can turn those off. Exactly what's sent.

One permission.

Accessibility, so it can arrange windows. Others only when you turn a feature on.

Smart, on device.

Sentences, predictions and suggestions run on your Mac. The optional assistant uses Claude only if you add your own API key.

Crash reports stay put.

Crash and hang reports stay on your Mac. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it.

FAQ

Good questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature, including docking, tab groups, saved views, scripting and every display, with no trial, subscription or window limits. The source is on GitHub under the Functional Source License.

Is it safe to install?

Yes. Every release is signed with Ashforde's Apple Developer ID and notarised by Apple, so macOS opens it without warnings. Safe to install shows how to check a download yourself.

Will it fight my other window tools?

It can, so quit other window managers and turn off Stage Manager. Settings → General → Mac setup checks for both.

What happens to my windows if I quit?

Every window comes back on screen. Next time Monorail starts, your layout returns.

Does it work with multiple displays, projectors and Sidecar?

Yes: each display gets its own strip, an unplugged display's windows come back when you reconnect, and Sidecar iPads fit every slide.

Which languages?

English, German, French, Spanish, Japanese, Chinese (Simplified), Portuguese, Korean and Italian.

I use AeroSpace, yabai or Rectangle. Can I bring my setup?

Yes. Settings → Basics → Import Settings… brings over your shortcuts, gaps, floating apps and workspace rules.

Is there a Windows or Linux version?

Not yet. Both are on the way: tell us which you'd use, and watch the repository to hear when they're ready.

Can I script it?

Yes: monorail:// links, the monorail command-line tool, a local API, an MCP server for AI assistants, and AppleScript or JavaScript, with a gallery of scripts to start from.

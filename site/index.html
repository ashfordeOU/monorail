Every window rides one rail.

Monorail is a scrolling window manager for your Mac. New windows slide in next to the one you're using, and nothing else ever shrinks. Tell it what you want in plain words. Free and open source.

Download for Mac View on GitHub
brew tap ashfordeOU/monorail https://github.com/ashfordeOU/monorail && brew install --cask monorail

Why people switch

🚝 Nothing shrinks

New windows join the strip instead of squeezing the rest. Your editor stays the size you left it.

🗣️ Say it

“Slack and Mail side by side on 3.” “Safari two thirds.” “How do I keep my Mac awake?” All on your Mac, no cloud.

🤖 Made for AI agents

A chime when Claude Code or Codex waits for your approval. Assistants can drive Monorail over MCP.

🛡️ Private when sharing

Private windows step aside the moment you share your screen in Zoom, Teams or Meet.

☕ Stay Awake

Keep your Mac awake for an hour, while downloading, with the lid closed, or by itself with triggers.

🎨 27 themes

Classics, seasonal themes, Seasons that follows the year, and one-of-a-kind looks.

Every workspace on one canvas

⌃⌥O shows all of your workspaces and slides. Drag windows between them, pair them, close them.

Privacy

Private by design.

Monorail and Keeper talk only on your Mac. Nothing about your windows or files leaves it.

  • Local only. Through localhost, or a folder only Ashforde's apps share (an App Group).
  • No server. There is nothing in between to send anything to.
  • Your windows stay yours. Nothing about your windows or files is sent anywhere.

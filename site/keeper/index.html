Monorail + Keeper

Two Mac apps from Ashforde. Each works on its own, and together they know about each other: no fighting over windows, one Stay Awake, one set of rules.

Monorail

A scrolling window manager. Every window gets a slide on a strip that runs past the screen edges, so new windows slide in without squeezing the others. Free, with every feature.

Download Monorail

Keeper

Your files, mail, calendar, notes and Mac tools in one suite, with a Dynamic Island, widgets and screen recording. Local-first.

Get Keeper

Better together

Keeper's panels stay put

Monorail leaves Keeper's Island, Glance, widgets and other panels alone: they're never tiled, moved or pushed off screen. Keeper's main window tiles like any other.

One Stay Awake

Start Stay Awake in either app and only one of them holds the Mac awake. While Keeper keeps the Mac awake, a session you start in Monorail goes to Keeper.

Lid closed, safely

Keep a MacBook running with the lid closed under the same rules in both apps: only while plugged in unless you choose otherwise, and off when it gets hot or in Low Power Mode. In Monorail's download version.

Each shows the other

Under Also from Ashforde, Monorail's menu shows what Keeper is doing and opens it, and Keeper's menu shows Monorail.

Signed and notarised

From version 1.7.16, Monorail is signed with Ashforde's Apple Developer ID and notarised by Apple, so it opens without warnings.

Local-first

They talk to each other only on your Mac, through localhost or a folder only Ashforde's apps share, and nothing about your windows or files leaves it.

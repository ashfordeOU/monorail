About Monorail

In one line

Monorail puts your whole Mac on one rail: a free scrolling window manager where new windows never squeeze the rest, with a Dashboard for everything your Mac is doing, AI plan limits, a clipboard, one-click switches, meeting notes, Stay Awake and Evening Mode built in.

In three paragraphs

Monorail by Ashforde® is a free Mac app built around a scrolling window manager. Most tiling window managers shrink every window when a new one opens. Monorail puts each window on a slide in a horizontal strip that runs past the edges of the screen: a new window slides in beside the one you’re using, and nothing else changes size. Windows can be dragged onto each other to dock side by side or form tab groups across any apps, saved views return the screen to an arrangement with one shortcut, and plain-language commands such as “Slack and Mail side by side” are understood on the Mac.

Around the strip, Monorail is a companion for the whole Mac. A Dashboard shows processor, memory, graphics, disk, network, battery, fans and every app with 30 days of history, along with AI plan limits and context for Claude, Codex and other tools, world time and weather, and one-click switches and modes. A clipboard keeps images and finds the words in screenshots; Meeting Notes writes down calls on the Mac; and a live notch shows what’s happening while it happens.

Monorail is native Swift, free with no paid tier, needs no account and only the Accessibility permission, and imports AeroSpace, yabai and Rectangle setups. AI agents can drive it through MCP, a ready-made skill or its command line.

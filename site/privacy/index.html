Privacy

Monorail has no account, and nothing about your windows, files or what you do leaves your Mac. Last updated 3 November 2026.

Permissions

Monorail needs one permission, Accessibility, which macOS requires before any app can move other apps' windows. You can revoke it at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility. macOS asks for others only when you turn on the feature that needs one: Screen Recording (live previews and sharing one slide), Calendars (opening video calls), Microphone and Speech Recognition (voice commands, recognised on your Mac), Camera (the camera tile), Local Network (the phone remote and layout sync).

Window pictures and text are read on your Mac and never stored or sent anywhere.

What Monorail sends

  • Anonymous usage counts, once a day, so we know how many Macs run Monorail and what to improve: a random install ID (not tied to you, your Apple ID or your Mac's serial number), the Monorail and macOS versions, the Mac model, chip type and core count, a memory range, the number of displays, the system language, how Monorail was installed and which features are on. Never window titles, app names, file names, what you type or say, or anything from your screen. The server doesn't store IP addresses. Turn it off in Settings → Devices & Sharing → Trust → Share anonymous usage counts. The app shows exactly what's sent under What's Sent….
  • The list of known app quirks, a small file that helps Monorail handle apps that behave unusually.
  • Update checks. They can be turned off in Settings → General.

Anything else happens only if you turn it on: webhooks, the phone remote, layout sync, iCloud sync and the optional assistant. The assistant works only with your own Anthropic API key, and sends just the sentence it didn't understand, the names of open apps and Monorail's list of commands, straight to Anthropic.

Crash reports

Crash and hang reports stay on your Mac unless you choose to send them. After an unexpected quit, Monorail may ask what happened and open a GitHub issue for you to review: it contains the Monorail and macOS versions and where it crashed, never window titles, files, paths or names, and nothing is sent until you submit it.

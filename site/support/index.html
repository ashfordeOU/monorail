Support

Help with Monorail, the scrolling window manager for Mac.

Getting started

Open Monorail and allow Accessibility when asked: the welcome tour walks you through the rest. Choose Simple for the essentials, Power user for every setting, or Coming from another app to import your AeroSpace, yabai or Rectangle setup.

Settings shows the essentials at first. To see every section, turn on Show all settings at the bottom of the sidebar; the search field finds every setting either way.

Common questions

  • A window ended up off screen: choose Rescue Windows from the menu bar icon.
  • Monorail doesn't move windows: check that Monorail is switched on in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility. If it is, switch it off and on again. After updating to 1.7.16 or later, macOS asks once more because the signature changed: remove Monorail from the list, then open it again.
  • I don't like the bar at the bottom: Settings → Appearance → Slider bar. Show it always, only while sliding, at the top of the screen, or turn it off.
  • Find any setting: type in the search field at the top of Settings, or ask in the command palette (⌃⌥/).

Report a problem or ask a question

In the app, Settings → Devices & Sharing → Report a Problem… starts an email to us with your Mac and Monorail version filled in. For ideas, choose Send Feedback… from the menu bar icon. Or write to contact@ashforde.org yourself. We reply by email.

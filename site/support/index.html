Support

How can we help?

Answers to the questions people ask most, and a person at the other end of contact@ashforde.org when you need one.

Start here

Getting started

Open Monorail and allow Accessibility when asked: the welcome tour does the rest. Choose Simple for the essentials, Power user for every setting, or Coming from another app to import your AeroSpace, yabai or Rectangle setup.

Find anything

Every setting, searchable

Settings shows the essentials first; turn on Show all settings at the bottom of the sidebar for the rest. The search field, and the command palette (⌃⌥/), find any setting either way.

Talk to us

Report a problem

Settings → Devices & Sharing → Report a Problem… starts an email with your Mac and Monorail version filled in. For ideas, choose Send Feedback… from the menu bar icon. We reply by email.

Windows

A window ended up off screen.

Choose Rescue Windows from the Monorail menu. Every window comes back onto the screen you’re looking at.

Monorail doesn’t move windows.

Check that Monorail is switched on in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility. If it is, switch it off and on again. If you updated from a version before 1.7.16, macOS asks once more because the signature changed: remove Monorail from the list, then open it again.

I don’t like the bar above the Dock.

Settings → Appearance → Slider bar: show it always, only while sliding, at the top of the screen, as one app icon per slide, or turn it off.

Can I slide with my mouse?

Yes. Hold ⌥ and turn the wheel, or turn on Settings → Motion & Input → “A mouse’s thumb wheel slides the strip” for mice with a sideways wheel, such as the MX Master. The back and forward buttons step through where you’ve been.

Another window manager is fighting Monorail.

Quit other window managers first; Settings checks for them and says which. Stage Manager is fine to keep: Monorail pauses while it’s on.

Can I bring my AeroSpace, yabai or Rectangle setup?

Yes. Settings → Basics → Import Settings… brings over your shortcuts, gaps, floating apps and workspace rules.

Dashboard and AI

How do I add my Claude, Codex or API accounts?

Open the Dashboard, choose AI Usage, then Add Account. Add as many as you like. Keys are kept in your Keychain. Turn on live plan limits to ask Claude and Codex for them with their own sign-in.

When do AI limit notifications arrive?

When a plan limit passes 80% and 95%, and when it resets. Change the levels in AI Usage’s settings.

Which app is draining my battery?

Click the Monorail icon: a draining app moves to the top with Quit on it. The Battery page in the Dashboard shows use by app over 30 days.

How do I connect an AI agent?

Settings → Automation → Local API, then Copy MCP Server Config, Save Agent Skill… or Copy Agent Instructions. The agents guide has the details.

Everyday tools

Does the clipboard keep passwords?

No. Copies from password managers are never kept, and anything that looks like a key, token or card number stays hidden until you click to show it. You can also choose apps whose copies are never kept.

Meeting Notes only hears me, not the others.

To hear everyone else too, Meeting Notes listens to your Mac’s sound, which needs the Screen Recording permission. Allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording. Without it, only your microphone is written down.

Where are the summaries in Meeting Notes?

Summaries come from Apple’s on-device model, on macOS 26 with Apple Intelligence turned on. On earlier versions you still get the full transcript and the action items.

How do I turn on the live notch?

On a MacBook with a notch, open the Dashboard’s Switches page and turn on “Show what’s happening beside the notch”. Sharing, a meeting, a timer, charging or an AI limit running low then shows beside it while it’s happening.

The menu bar panel is hard to read over my wallpaper.

Settings → Appearance → Panel background: Automatic, Frosted or Solid. Reduce Transparency in System Settings always makes it solid, and Increase Contrast gives it a firmer edge.

Can Monorail start with my Mac?

Yes: Settings → General → Launch Monorail at login.

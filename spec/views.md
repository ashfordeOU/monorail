# Saved views, back and forward

- A **view** is a workspace on a display, how far the strip is scrolled, and the window in front.
- Each display has nine **saved view** slots, numbered 1–9, kept across restarts. Saving a view
  overwrites its slot. Going to an empty slot does nothing except a notice.
- **Back and forward** work like a browser: switching workspace or jumping to a saved view records
  the view you left (up to 50); going back moves the current view to the forward list; any new
  jump clears the forward list.
- Restoring a view switches to its workspace (on its own display), restores the scroll position and
  focuses its window if it still exists.
